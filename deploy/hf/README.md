# PCP Digital Portal (demo)

A working demo of the Pharmacy Council of Pakistan's digitisation guide: institution onboarding, Pharm.D NOC and
accreditation cases, faculty and student registries, inspections, Council, payments and the public check.

All people, CNICs and institutions are fictional. Payments, NADRA, HEC, SMS and email are simulated.
The demo resets to its starting data whenever the Space restarts.

Start at `/coverage` to see every point of the working guide and try each one in a click.
